# Analytics

Open **ProductBoxes → Analytics** to see how your boxes did in the last **7, 30 or 90 days**.

## Key numbers

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Revenue from boxes** | Total revenue from orders containing boxes. |
| **Boxes sold** | Number of boxes ordered. |
| **Average box value** | Revenue divided by boxes sold. |
| **Discount given** | Savings passed on to shoppers. |
| **Builder conversion** | Share of shoppers who opened the builder and bought. |

Turn on **Compare to previous period** to see the change for each number.

## Charts and tables

* **Revenue from boxes, daily** shows trends at a glance.
* **Shopper journey** follows the funnel: *Opened the builder → Added an item → Reached a discount → Added box to cart*.
* **Most picked products** shows what goes in boxes most.
* **Box performance** compares your boxes side by side.

Click **Export CSV** to take the data into a spreadsheet.

## Privacy

Analytics are optional and stay in **your** database. See [Privacy and data](../reference/privacy.md).

{% hint style="info" %}
Turn analytics off in **Settings → Data and privacy → Collect box analytics**, or with the `mmbb_track_events` filter.
{% endhint %}
