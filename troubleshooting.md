# Troubleshooting

## The box does not show on my page

1. Confirm the box is **Published** in **ProductBoxes → Boxes**.
2. Check that **Box builders are on** in **Settings**.
3. If you used a shortcode, confirm the `id` matches the box.
4. Clear any page or caching-plugin cache.

## A product is missing from the grid

* **Variable products** are left out of category and tag selections. Pick the variations by hand instead.
* Out-of-stock products show as **Sold out**, or are hidden if **Hide sold-out products** is on.

## Add to cart stays locked

The box has fewer items than **Minimum to check out**, or a fixed-size box has empty slots.

## A coupon does not apply

**Allow coupons on this box** is off by default. Turn it on for that box, or in Settings.

## The preview looks different from my site

The preview uses your active theme. If you changed themes, reopen the editor. With **Match my site** on, colours and fonts follow the theme.

## "Copy didn't work"

Your browser blocked clipboard access. Select the shortcode and copy it by hand.
