# FAQ

{% hint style="info" %}
Want to see it first? Try the [live demos](demos.md). Need help? [Chat live or hire an expert](support.md).
{% endhint %}

<details>

<summary>Is ProductBoxes really free?</summary>

Yes. All features are in the free plugin. There are no premium upgrades, subscriptions or account requirements.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I create a mix and match box?</summary>

Create a box, select eligible products, configure the discount, choose a template and publish it with a page, block or shortcode. See the [Quick start](getting-started/quick-start.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can customers choose product variations?</summary>

Yes. Simple products and individual variations are supported, so shoppers can mix sizes, colours and flavours. Pick variations by hand. Category and tag selections include simple products only.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I offer fixed-price bundles like "any 3 for $18"?</summary>

Yes. Choose **Bundle price** and set the number of items and the total price.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I offer bulk or tiered discounts?</summary>

Yes. Use **Tiered discount** to give bigger discounts as shoppers add more items.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I sell gift boxes with a message?</summary>

Yes. Enable gift wrap and a gift message. See [Gift boxes](guides/gift-boxes.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Does it work with the Cart and Checkout blocks, HPOS and WooPayments?</summary>

Yes to all three. See [Compatibility](reference/compatibility.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Will updating to 2.0 delete my data?</summary>

No. Campaigns, boxes, settings, orders and analytics are preserved through versioned migrations.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Does it use external services?</summary>

No. Everything is bundled locally. See [Privacy and data](reference/privacy.md).

</details>
